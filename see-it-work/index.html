The story

‘What environmental racism looks like’: Protest targets University of Michigan computing center

By Brian Allnutt · Planet Detroit · October 2, 2026

A University of Michigan regent joined residents, labor unions and local officials at an October 1 protest against the university’s planned $1.25-billion computing center in Ypsilanti Township, held before a university town hall on the project. Neighbors said the university had not come to their doors to answer questions about power, water and noise.

The Toolbox read the story and found the public bodies deciding on the project, the meetings coming up, the officials to contact and the organizations already working on it — then an editor checked every line before publishing.

What’s in the box
  • Why it matters and who’s deciding — two plain sentences.
  • Upcoming meetings you can attend, with agendas and calendar links.
  • Open comment periods and deadlines, while they are still open.
  • Elected officials for the place the story is about, with email and phone.
  • What you can do — concrete actions, and a tick to say you did one.
  • Organizations to follow already working on the issue.

Meetings drop out of the box once they have passed, so what you see is what is still ahead.
